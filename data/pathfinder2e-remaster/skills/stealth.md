# [Stealth (Dex)](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=48)

**Source** [*Player Core pg. 244*](https://2e.aonprd.com/Sources.aspx?ID=216)
You are skilled at avoiding detection, allowing you to slip past foes, hide, or conceal an item.

# Stealth Untrained Actions

## [Conceal an Object](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2403) [one-action]

[Manipulate](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=104) [Secret](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=142)
**Source** [*Player Core pg. 244*](https://2e.aonprd.com/Sources.aspx?ID=216)

---

You hide a small object on your person (such as a weapon of light Bulk). When you try to sneak a concealed object past someone who might notice it, the GM rolls your [Stealth](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=15) check and compares it to this passive observer's Perception DC. Once the GM rolls your check for a concealed object, that same result is used no matter how many passive observers you try to sneak it past. If a creature is specifically searching you for an item, it can attempt a Perception check against your Stealth DC (finding the object on success).

You can also conceal an object somewhere other than your person, such as among undergrowth or in a secret compartment within a piece of furniture. In this case, characters [Seeking](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=84) in an area compare their Perception check results to your Stealth DC to determine whether they find the object.

**Success** The object remains undetected.
**Failure** The searcher finds the object.

## [Hide](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2404) [one-action]

[Secret](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=142)
**Source** [*Player Core pg. 244*](https://2e.aonprd.com/Sources.aspx?ID=216)

---

You huddle behind [cover or greater cover](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=459) or deeper into concealment to become [hidden](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=22), rather than [observed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=27). The GM rolls your [Stealth](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=15) check in secret and compares the result to the Perception DC of each creature you're observed by but that you have cover or greater cover against or are concealed from. You get a +2 circumstance bonus to your check if you have standard cover (or +4 from greater cover).

**Success** If the creature could see you, you're now hidden from it instead of observed. If you were hidden from or [undetected](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=39) by the creature, you retain that condition.

If you successfully become hidden to a creature but then cease to have cover or greater cover against it or be [concealed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=4) from it, you become observed again. You cease being hidden if you do anything except Hide, [Sneak](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=63), or Step. If you attempt to Strike a creature, the creature remains [off-guard](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=58) against that attack, and you then become observed. If you do anything else, you become observed just before you act unless the GM determines otherwise. The GM might allow you to perform a particularly unobtrusive action without being noticed, possibly requiring another Stealth check.

If a creature uses [Seek](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=84) to make you observed by it, you must successfully Hide to become hidden from it again.

## [Sneak](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2405) [one-action]

[Move](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=114) [Secret](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=142)
**Source** [*Player Core pg. 245*](https://2e.aonprd.com/Sources.aspx?ID=216)

---

You attempt to move to another place while becoming or staying [undetected](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=39). Stride up to half your Speed. (You can use Sneak while [Burrowing](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=93), [Climbing](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=33), [Flying](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=94), or [Swimming](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=39) instead of Striding if you have the corresponding movement type; you must move at half that Speed.)

At the end of your movement, the GM rolls your [Stealth](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=15) check in secret and compares the result to the Perception DC of each creature you were [hidden](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=22) from or undetected by at the start of your movement. If you have [cover or greater cover](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=459) from the creature throughout your Stride, you gain the +2 circumstance bonus from cover (or +4 from greater cover) to your Stealth check. Because you're moving, the bonus increase from [Taking Cover](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=90) doesn't apply. You don't get to roll against a creature if, at the end of your movement, you neither are [concealed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=4) from it nor have cover or greater cover against it. You automatically become observed by such a creature.

**Success** You're undetected by the creature during your movement and remain undetected by the creature at the end of it.

You become observed as soon as you do anything other than [Hide](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=62), Sneak, or Step. If you attempt to Strike a creature, the creature remains [off-guard](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=58) against that attack, and you then become observed. If you do anything else, you become observed just before you act unless the GM determines otherwise. The GM might allow you to perform a particularly unobtrusive action without being noticed, possibly requiring another Stealth check. If you speak or make a deliberate loud noise, you become hidden instead of undetected.

If a creature uses [Seek](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=84) and you become hidden to it as a result, you must Sneak if you want to become undetected by that creature again.
**Failure** A telltale sound or other sign gives your position away, though you still remain unseen. You're hidden from the creature throughout your movement and remain so.
**Critical Failure** You're spotted! You're observed by the creature throughout your movement and remain so. If you're [invisible](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=26) and were hidden from the creature, instead of being observed you're hidden throughout your movement and remain so.

### Being Stealthy

If you want to sneak around when there are creatures that can see you, you can use a combination of [Hide](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=62) and [Sneak](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=63) to do so.

- First, **Hide** behind something (either by taking advantage of [cover](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=459) or having the [concealed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=4) condition due to fog, a spell, or a similar effect). A successful Stealth check makes you [hidden](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=22), though the creatures still know roughly where you are.

- Second, now that you're hidden, you can **Sneak**. That means you can move at half your Speed and attempt another Stealth check. If it's successful, you're now [undetected](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=39). That means the creatures don't know which square you're in anymore.

If you were approaching creatures that didn't know you were there, you could begin Sneaking right away, since they didn't know your location to start with. Some actions can cause you to become [observed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=27) again, but they're mostly what you'd expect: standing out in the open, attacking someone, making a bunch of noise, and so forth. Unobtrusive actions, such as Recalling Knowledge or reloading a weapon, allow you to remain hidden or undetected. If you Strike someone after successfully Hiding or Sneaking, though, they're [off-guard](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=58) to that Strike.

Creatures can try to find you using the [Seek](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=84) action.

Three conditions explain the states of detection. Remember that these conditions are relative to each creature—you can be observed by one creature while hidden to another and undetected by a third. [**Observed**](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=27): You're in the creature's clear view. [**Hidden**](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=22): The creature knows your location but can't see you. [**Undetected**](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=39): The creature doesn't know your location.

### Unobservable Stealth

In some cases, it can be impossible for a creature to fully observe you. Typically this happens if you're [invisible](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=26), the observer is [blinded](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=1), or you're in [darkness](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=409) and the creature can't see in darkness. In such cases, any critical failure you roll on a check to Sneak is a failure instead. You also continue to be [undetected](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=39) if you lose [cover or greater cover](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=459) against or are no longer [concealed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=4) from such a creature.
