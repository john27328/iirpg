# [Arcana (Int)](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=35)

**Source** [*Player Core pg. 234*](https://2e.aonprd.com/Sources.aspx?ID=216)
Arcana measures how much you know about arcane magic and creatures.

- **[Recall Knowledge](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=24&General=true)** about arcane theories; magic traditions; creatures of arcane significance (like [constructs](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=563), [beasts](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=547), and [elementals](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=587)); and the [Astral Plane](https://2e.aonprd.com/Planes.aspx?ID=8), Elemental Planes, and [the Netherworld](https://2e.aonprd.com/Planes.aspx?ID=36).

## Arcana Trained Actions

- **[Decipher Writing](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=20&General=true)** about arcane theory.

- **[Identify Magic](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=22&General=true)**, particularly magic of the [arcane](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=1) tradition.

- **[Learn a Spell](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=23&General=true)** from the [arcane](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=1) tradition.

# Arcana Trained Actions

## [Borrow an Arcane Spell](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2373)

[Concentrate](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=32) [Exploration](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=595)
**Source** [*Player Core pg. 234*](https://2e.aonprd.com/Sources.aspx?ID=216)

---

If you're an arcane spellcaster who prepares spells, you can attempt to prepare a spell from someone else's arcane spellbook, arcane witch familiar, or the like. The GM sets the DC for the check based on the spell's rank and rarity; it's typically a bit easier than [Learning the Spell](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=25).

**Success** You prepare the borrowed spell as part of your normal spell preparation.
**Failure** You fail to prepare the spell, but the spell slot remains available for you to prepare a different spell. You can't try to borrow this spell again until the next time you prepare spells.
