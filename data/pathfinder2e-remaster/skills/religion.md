# [Religion (Wis)](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=46)

**Source** [*Player Core pg. 244*](https://2e.aonprd.com/Sources.aspx?ID=216)
The secrets of deities, dogma, faith, and the realms of divine creatures both sublime and sinister are open to you. You also understand how magic works, though your training imparts a religious slant to that knowledge.

- **[Recall Knowledge](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=24&General=true)** about divine agents, the finer points of theology, obscure myths regarding a faith, and creatures of religious significance (like [celestials](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=551), [fiends](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=600), and [undead](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=160)), [Creation's Forge](https://2e.aonprd.com/Planes.aspx?ID=27), [the Void](https://2e.aonprd.com/Planes.aspx?ID=28), and the Outer Sphere.

## Religion Trained Actions

- **[Decipher Writing](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=20&General=true)** of a religious nature, including allegories, homilies, and proverbs.

- **[Identify Magic](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=22&General=true)**, particularly magic of the [divine](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=2) tradition.

- **[Learn a Spell](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=23&General=true)** from the [divine](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=2) tradition.
