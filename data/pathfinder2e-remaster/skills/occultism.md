# [Occultism (Int)](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=44)

**Source** [*Player Core pg. 243*](https://2e.aonprd.com/Sources.aspx?ID=216)
You know a great deal about ancient philosophies, esoteric lore, obscure mysticism, and supernatural creatures.

- **[Recall Knowledge](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=24&General=true)** about ancient mysteries; obscure philosophies; creatures of occult significance (like [aberrations](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=523), [spirits](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=149), and [oozes](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=665)); and esoteric planes like the [Astral Plane](https://2e.aonprd.com/Planes.aspx?ID=8), [Creation's Forge](https://2e.aonprd.com/Planes.aspx?ID=27), the [Ethereal Plane](https://2e.aonprd.com/Planes.aspx?ID=9), [the Netherworld](https://2e.aonprd.com/Planes.aspx?ID=36), and [the Void](https://2e.aonprd.com/Planes.aspx?ID=28).

## Occultism Trained Actions

- **[Decipher Writing](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=20&General=true)** on occult topics, including complex metaphysical systems, syncretic principles, obscure philosophies, and incoherent ramblings.

- **[Identify Magic](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=22&General=true)**, particularly magic of the [occult](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=3) tradition.

- **[Learn a Spell](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=23&General=true)** from the [occult](https://2e.aonprd.com/SpellLists.aspx?Tradition=3) tradition.
